# 06 · Unsubscribe 🚪

> **Changes Gmail?** No. **You** click the links.

Unsubscribing stops mail at the source, so it never reaches you or your filters.

## Prompt

```
For every sender I marked "unsubscribe", open its most recent email and
give me a list with: sender, how many emails it sent, and either
- the unsubscribe link from the email, or
- for social networks, where in the app's settings to turn email off.

Skip anything in the suspicious group: for those, mark as spam instead.
Don't click any links yourself.
```

## Why you click, not Claude

Unsubscribe links come from inside emails, and some lead to pages that ask you to confirm. Clicking is your call, and it's one click each.

## Shortcuts

- 🖱️ In Gmail, open the email and click **Unsubscribe** next to the sender's name at the top. Gmail handles it for you.
- 📵 **Social networks:** each email link only stops one kind of notification. It's faster to go to the app's **Settings → Notifications → Email** and pick "account and security only".
- 🚩 **Never** unsubscribe from a suspicious email. It confirms your address is real. Mark it as spam.
