# 06 · Unsubscribe

**Changes your mailbox:** No. You click the links yourself.

## Prompt

```
For every sender marked "unsubscribe", open its most recent email and give
me a list: sender, and either the unsubscribe link or, for social apps,
where in the app's settings to turn email notifications off.
Skip anything in the suspicious group.
```

## Safety

- Never click unsubscribe in a suspicious email. It can confirm your address is live. Mark it as spam instead.
- Social networks (Facebook, LinkedIn…) are better silenced in their notification settings than by unsubscribe links.
