# 05 · Clean up, in batches

**Changes your mailbox:** Yes, after you confirm each batch.

## Prompt

```
Carry out my confirmed decisions in this order. Stop after each step and
show me what you did before continuing.

1. Create these labels if missing: "Newsletters", "Receipts & Bills",
   "Notices", "Social".
2. "label" senders: apply the matching label and remove them from the inbox.
3. "archive" senders: remove from the inbox only. Do NOT trash or delete.
4. "spam" senders: mark as spam.

Do the first step on at most 20 threads so I can check it in Gmail before
you do the rest. Never trash or permanently delete anything. Leave "keep"
senders untouched.
```

## Why archive and not delete

Archived mail stays searchable under **All Mail**, so nothing is lost if a sender turns out to matter.
